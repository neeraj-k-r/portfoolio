# Portfoolio SEO Ranking Master Strategy: Dominating "Portfolio Maker"

## 1. Executive Summary
"Portfolio maker" is a high-volume, high-intent global search term. This document outlines Portfoolio's product-led strategy to rank #1 against legacy competitors like Wix, Canva, and Adobe Portfolio.

## 2. The Product-Led Backlink Engine (The Carrd Playbook)
- Every user portfolio on `username.portfoolio.me` carries a lightweight, elegant backlink:
  `Made with Portfoolio — Portfolio Maker` pointing to `https://portfoolio.me/`.
- As users share their portfolios on LinkedIn, GitHub, X/Twitter, and resumes, thousands of natural, relevant backlinks flow into `portfoolio.me`.
- Every developer who displays the `[![Portfolio](https://portfoolio.me/badge.svg)](https://portfoolio.me)` badge creates a direct, do-follow connection from GitHub's domain to Portfoolio.
